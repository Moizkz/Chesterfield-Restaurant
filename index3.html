Our Story

Rooted in Manchester

Founded in 2026

In the heart of Manchester, The Chesterfield was born from a passion for modern British cooking, warm hospitality, and bringing people together over good food and drink.

What began as a small neighbourhood bistro has grown into a destination for timeless dishes, seasonal flavours, and unforgettable evenings.

🍃 Seasonal Ingredients
Fresh, local produce sourced daily from trusted farms across the UK.
🌾 Locally Sourced
